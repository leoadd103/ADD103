Mozzarella Sticks

Coat mozzarella sticks in flour, egg, and seasoned breadcrumbs. Fry until golden and crispy, then serve with warm marinara sauce.

Chicken Caesar Salad

Roast seasoned chicken until fully cooked, then slice it. Serve over romaine lettuce with croutons, Parmesan cheese, and Caesar dressing.

Caramel Meringue Dessert

Prepare a light meringue dessert and layer it into a round shape. Serve with caramel sauce and fresh fruit for a sweet finish.
